Common questions

Is depression therapy covered by Medicare?+

Yes. Behavioral health is covered by Medicare. Talk therapy for depression is part of that. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance.

Is a diagnosis needed first?+

No. Nobody needs a diagnosis to book a call. The therapist and the member decide together what to work on and how often to meet.

Can I do it by phone?+

Yes. Sessions are by phone or by video from home, whichever is easier. A regular phone call needs no computer.

Is it common to start therapy after 65?+

Yes. Total Life’s therapists work with adults 65 and older, and first-time members are welcome. Members set the pace and choose what to share.

What happens after I book?+

We call you at the time you picked. We check your coverage and answer your questions. If you want to go ahead, we find you a therapist who works with older adults.
