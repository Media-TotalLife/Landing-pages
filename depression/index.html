Common questions.

Is depression therapy covered by Medicare?+

Behavioral health is covered by Medicare, and Total Life is an enrolled Medicare provider and accepts Medicare and most supplemental plans. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance.

On the call we check the plan and say what it covers.

Is a diagnosis needed?+

No diagnosis is needed to book a call. The therapist and the member decide together what to work on and how often to meet.

Can I do it by phone?+

Yes. Sessions are by phone or by video, whichever is easier. Nothing to install, and you talk from home.

Is it unusual to start therapy later in life?+

No. Total Life's therapists work with adults 65 and older, and first-time members are welcome. Members set the pace and choose what to share.

What happens after I book?+

A member of our care team calls at the time you chose. We check coverage and answer questions, and if you want to go ahead we match you with a therapist.
