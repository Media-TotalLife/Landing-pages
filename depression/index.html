Is depression therapy covered by Medicare?+

Yes. Medicare covers talk therapy for depression and other mental health conditions. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance.

Total Life is an enrolled Medicare provider. On the call we check your plan and tell you what it covers.

Is it only for people with a diagnosis?+

No. No diagnosis is needed to book a call or to start therapy.

The therapist and the member decide together what to work on and how often to meet.

Can I do it by phone?+

Yes. Sessions can be by phone or by video call. Whatever is easier.

No apps to download. No driving. You talk from your own home.

I've never talked to anyone about this.+

Many members hadn't either. That is common at any age.

Total Life therapists work with older adults every day. Members set the pace and choose what to share.

What happens after I book?+

A member of our care team calls at the time you chose. They check your Medicare coverage and answer your questions.

If you want to go ahead, we match you with a therapist. There is no pressure at any step.
