Questions

Good questions. Honest answers.

Is therapy covered by Medicare?+

Medicare Part B covers talk therapy with a licensed therapist, and Total Life is an enrolled Medicare provider.

Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance.

What will I pay?+

It depends on your plan, and we tell you the exact amount before you start.

With Original Medicare alone the share is often around 20% of each session; with a supplemental plan it is often covered in full.

Do I need a computer?+

No, a regular phone call works.

Video is available for anyone who prefers it, and we help set it up.

Is what I say private?+

What you share with your therapist is protected by law and stays between you.

We never sell your information.

What happens after I book?+

A member of our care team calls at the time you chose, goes over your coverage and answers your questions.

If you decide to go ahead, we match you with a therapist and set up your first session.
