Questions

Good questions. Honest answers.

Is therapy really covered by Medicare?+

Yes. Medicare Part B covers talk therapy with a licensed therapist. Total Life is an enrolled Medicare provider.

Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance. We check your exact plan before your first session.

What will I pay?+

It depends on your plan. With Original Medicare alone, you may pay a share of each session, often around 20%.

With a supplemental plan, many members pay nothing. We tell you the exact amount before you start. No surprises.

Do I need a computer?+

No. Many members meet their therapist by phone. A regular phone call works just fine.

If you would like video, we can help you set that up. You choose what feels easiest.

I've never done therapy. Is it for someone like me?+

Yes. Most of our members are trying therapy for the first time. There is nothing you need to know going in.

Your therapist works with older adults every day. You set the pace. You can talk about whatever matters to you.

Is what I say private?+

Yes. What you share with your therapist stays between you, protected by law. We do not tell your family or your doctor unless you ask us to.

We never sell your information.

What happens after I check my coverage?+

A real person from our team calls you within one business day. We go over your coverage together and answer your questions.

If you want to go ahead, we match you with a therapist. Your first session is usually within a week or two.
