Common questions

Is grief counseling covered by Medicare?+

Yes. Talk therapy is part of what Medicare calls behavioral health. Behavioral health is covered by insurance. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance. With Original Medicare alone most members pay a share. We check your coverage on the call.

How soon after a loss can someone start?+

Whenever they choose. Some people come in the first weeks after a loss. Others come years later, around an anniversary or a holiday.

Is it only for the loss of a spouse?+

No. Grief counseling covers the loss of a friend, a sibling, an adult child or a pet as well. The therapist works with whatever loss a person brings.

Can I have sessions by phone, with no computer?+

Yes. A regular phone call works, and no computer is needed. Anyone who prefers video gets help setting it up.

Can I change the time after I book?+

Yes. Call 1-800-567-5433 and we will move it for you.
