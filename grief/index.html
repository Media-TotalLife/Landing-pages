What grief counseling covers

Grief takes many shapes.

  • Losing a spouse or partner

    The loss of a husband, wife or long-time partner.

  • Losing a friend, sibling or adult child

    Losses that others often overlook.

  • The first year, anniversaries and holidays

    Dates that bring grief back, sometimes years later.

  • Grief that arrives with retirement or a move

    Leaving a career, a home or a community is a loss too.

  • Caring for someone through a long illness

    Grief that begins before a death and continues after it.

  • Losing a pet

    A companion of many years is a real loss.

Questions

Good questions. Honest answers.

Is grief counseling covered by Medicare?+

Yes. Medicare Part B covers counseling with a licensed therapist, and grief counseling is part of that. Total Life is an enrolled Medicare provider.

Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance. We check your exact plan on the call, before any session.

How soon after a loss can someone start?+

Whenever they choose. There is no right time and no waiting period.

Some members call in the first weeks after a loss. Others call years later, around an anniversary or a holiday. Both are common.

Is it only for the loss of a spouse?+

No. Members come to grief counseling after losing a friend, a sibling, an adult child or a pet, and after losses such as retirement or leaving a family home.

Your therapist works with whatever loss you bring.

Can I do it by phone?+

Yes. Many members meet their therapist by a regular phone call. No computer is needed.

If you would like video, we can help you set it up. You choose what feels easiest.

What happens after I book?+

A real person from our care team calls you at the time you picked. We go over your coverage together and answer your questions.

If you want to go ahead, we match you with a therapist. The first session is usually within a week or two.
