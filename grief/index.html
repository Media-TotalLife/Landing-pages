Common questions

Is grief counseling covered by Medicare?+

Yes. Grief counseling is talk therapy, and behavioral health is covered by Medicare. Total Life is an enrolled Medicare provider. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance.

How soon after a loss can someone start?+

Whenever they choose. Some come in the first weeks after a loss, others years later around an anniversary or a holiday.

Is it only for the loss of a spouse?+

No. Members come after losing a friend, a sibling, an adult child or a pet. The therapist works with whatever loss the member brings.

Can I do it by phone?+

Yes. A regular phone call works, and no computer is needed. Anyone who prefers video gets help setting it up.

What happens after I book?+

A member of our care team calls at the time you chose. We check coverage and answer questions, and if you want to go ahead we match you with a therapist.
