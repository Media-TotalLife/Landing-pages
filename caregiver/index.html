Questions

Good questions. Honest answers.

What if I'm booking for a parent who says they're fine?+

That happens often. We call you first, listen to what you have noticed, then decide together how to bring it up, gently and in words they will hear.

If they are not ready, we do not push. They decide what comes next. For many families the answer is "not yet" the first time, and "okay" a little later.

Will you call my family member without telling me?+

No. Nothing happens without you. You tell us when and how to reach out, and whether you would like to be on the line. We only reach out with your okay, and we never surprise anyone with a cold call.

Is it really covered by Medicare?+

Yes. Talk therapy is a covered Medicare benefit, and Total Life is an enrolled Medicare provider. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance. With Original Medicare alone there is usually a small share to pay.

We check the plan before the first session, so there are no surprises.

Can family be part of the sessions?+

That is up to the member and their therapist. Some families join a first session together. Others stay in the loop through updates the member agrees to share. Sessions are private, like any medical care, and with consent we keep family posted on how things are going.

Is a computer needed?+

No. Most of our members meet their therapist by phone, a regular phone call at a regular time. Anyone who would like to try video gets walked through it step by step. If not, the phone works just as well.
