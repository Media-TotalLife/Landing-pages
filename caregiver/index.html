Signs it might be time

You're carrying a lot.

Most daughters and sons who call us have been noticing small things for a while. These are the ones we hear most.

  • She's quieter than usual.

  • She stopped doing the things she used to love.

  • She says “I'm fine” and changes the subject.

  • Sleep, appetite, or energy have changed.

  • You've started dreading the phone ringing.

None of these mean something is wrong. They mean it might be worth a conversation — and we can start it.

Questions

The things you're wondering.

What if Mom says she's fine?+

She might. A lot of moms do. That's why we call you first, not her. We listen to what you've noticed, then decide together how to bring it up — gently, in words she'll hear.

If she isn't ready, we don't push. She decides what's next. For many families the answer is "not yet" the first time, and "okay" a little later.

Will you call her without telling me?+

No. Nothing happens without you. You tell us when and how to reach out, and whether you'd like to be on the line. We only call Mom with your okay, and we never surprise her with a cold call.

Is it really covered by Medicare?+

Yes. Talk therapy and psychiatry are covered Medicare benefits, and Total Life is an enrolled Medicare provider. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance. With Original Medicare alone there is usually a small share to pay.

We check her plan with you before her first session, so there are no surprises for either of you.

Can I be part of her sessions?+

That's up to Mom and her therapist. Some families join a first session together. Others stay in the loop through updates she agrees to share. Her sessions are private, like any medical care — and with her consent, we'll keep you posted on how things are going.

What if she doesn't use a computer?+

She doesn't need one. Most of our members meet their therapist by phone — a regular phone call, at a regular time. If she'd like to try video, we'll walk her through it step by step. If not, the phone works just as well.

What does it cost me?+

There's no charge to you for the call, the matching, or care navigation. Her therapy is billed to her Medicare. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance, and we'll tell you both exactly what her plan covers before anything starts.
