Common questions

Can a family member help set this up?+

Yes. A family member can book the call and join it. The older adult decides whether to go ahead, and nothing starts until they say so.

Can a family member book on behalf of an older adult?+

Yes. The person booked for should know about the call and agree to it. We call the number on the booking at the chosen time; a family member is welcome to be on the line.

Is it covered by Medicare?+

Behavioral health is covered by Medicare, and Total Life is an enrolled Medicare provider. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance. With Original Medicare alone most members pay a share.

We go over the plan on the call, before any session.

Can family be part of the sessions?+

That is up to the member and their therapist. Sessions are private, and family is included only with the member's consent.

Is a computer needed?+

No. Sessions can be a regular phone call at a regular time. Anyone who prefers video gets help setting it up.
