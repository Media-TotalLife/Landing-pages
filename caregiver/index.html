What caregiver stress therapy helps with.

  • Caring for a spouse or partner through a long illness, and the strain it puts on the marriage.

  • Caring for a parent while running a home and keeping up with the rest of life.

  • Dementia caregiving, and the way the relationship changes as memory changes.

  • Anticipatory grief: grieving a person who is still here.

  • Making time for the carer's own health, rest and life outside caregiving.

Common questions

Is caregiver stress therapy covered by Medicare?+

Behavioral health is covered by Medicare, and Total Life is an enrolled Medicare provider. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance.

Is this for the caregiver or the person being cared for?+

For the caregiver, who is the member and the person the therapist sees. The person being cared for may have their own care separately.

Can sessions fit around caregiving?+

Sessions are by phone or video, from home, at a regular time each week. There is no travel and no waiting room.

Can a family member book with me?+

Yes, as long as the member knows about the call and agrees to it. A family member is welcome to book alongside the member and join the call.

What happens after I book?+

We call the number on the booking at the chosen time, go over coverage and talk about what matters most. Then we match the member with a therapist who works with older adults.
