Common questions

Is caregiver stress therapy covered by Medicare?+

Yes. Talk therapy is part of what Medicare calls behavioral health. Behavioral health is covered by insurance. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance. With Original Medicare alone most members pay a share. We check your coverage on the call.

Is this for the caregiver, or for the person being cared for?+

For the caregiver. The therapist meets with the caregiver. The person being cared for can see a therapist of their own, separately.

Can sessions fit around caregiving?+

Yes. Sessions are by phone or video, from home, at a regular time each week. There is no travel and no waiting room. A regular phone call works, and no computer is needed.

Can a family member book for me?+

Yes, as long as the person the call is for knows about it and agrees. A family member is welcome to book with you and to join the call.

Can I change the time after I book?+

Yes. Call 1-800-567-5433 and we will move it for you.
