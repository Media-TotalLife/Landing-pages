Questions

Is this a medical test?+

No. The four questions are a way to start a conversation. They are not a diagnosis.

Only a clinician can tell you more. If your answers worry you, a good next step is to talk to someone.

Is therapy covered by Medicare?+

Yes. Medicare covers talk therapy for mental and emotional health. Most members are covered up to 100% with Medicare + supplemental insurance.

When we call, we check your plan and tell you exactly what it covers. Total Life is an enrolled Medicare provider.

I've never talked to anyone about this.+

Most of our members hadn't either. That's very normal at any age.

Your therapist works with older adults every day. You set the pace. You choose what to share.

Can I do it by phone?+

Yes. You can meet by phone or by video call. Whatever is easier for you.

No apps to download. No driving. You talk from your own home.

What happens after I answer?+

A real person calls you within one business day. They check your Medicare coverage and answer your questions.

If you'd like to go ahead, we match you with a therapist. There's no pressure at any step.
